Our Story

The glow on Khojak Road everyone drives toward.

Anyone who has passed Old Pishin Stop after sunset knows it — the coals, the crowd, the smell of roasting sajji drifting across Khojak Road. Usmania is where Law College debates end, family dawats begin, and half of Quetta's celebrations eventually land.

The sajji roasts the Balochi way — low, patient, rock-salt honest. The ribs come off the tandoor crackling. And the Special Soup has been warming Quetta winters for years. From M.A. Jinnah Road to Model Town, one promise stays: full plates, fair prices, food that arrives hot and fast.
